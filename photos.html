Photos

Outside research I travel whenever I can, and I almost always end up next to water — an ocean, a lake, a river.

There is a reason it pulls at me. My working life is spent on water nobody can see: groundwater moving through sand and carbon at centimeters a day, known only through models and breakthrough curves. Above ground the same water does all the same things — it flows, it mixes, it carries things along, it gets held up — except you can stand in front of it and watch.

So these are mostly rivers, coastlines, and lakes, with whatever else caught my eye along the way.
