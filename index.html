Zijie Zheng

Environmental Engineering | Contaminant Transport | Groundwater Remediation | Numerical Simulation

I am a Ph.D. researcher in environmental engineering at The University of Texas at Austin. My research focuses on contaminant fate and transport in porous media, with current work on PFAS transport, reactive transport modeling, and groundwater remediation. I combine laboratory experiments and numerical simulations to understand contaminant behavior and support practical remediation design.
