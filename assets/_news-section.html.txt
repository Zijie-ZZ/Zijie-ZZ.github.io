Parked News section for the home page.

renders it. To bring News back, paste the block below into index.html just
before the closing </main> tag. The matching .news CSS is already in
css/style.css.

Newest first. To add an entry, copy one <li> block:

    <li>
      <span class="news-date">Mon YYYY</span>
      <span class="news-body">What happened.</span>
    </li>
